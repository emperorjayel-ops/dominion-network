About this app

Dominion Network, in partnership with the CEO Network (Bahamas), is a media and telecommunications app: streaming films, shows and music, live events, originals, and a Creator Pass for members to publish and stream their own work. Streaming and membership features are presented as a demo preview.

What data the app handles

  • Demo membership: membership and Creator Pass sign-ups in this app are demo/session-only. Information entered is held in the app session on your device and is not stored on our servers by this app.
  • Viewing activity: this app does not run third-party analytics or advertising trackers, and does not sell viewing data.
  • Creator content: content a creator previews in the app during a session is handled on-device for that session.
  • External links: linked partner and social sites have their own privacy policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data to anyone.
  • We do not run third-party advertising networks or ad trackers in this app.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, call logs, or device identifiers for advertising.

Payments and financial features

Membership and Creator Pass pricing shown in the app is a demo preview. No live payment is processed inside this app at this time.

Data retention

Session-only demo data is cleared when the session ends. Email correspondence is kept only as long as needed to respond.

Children

This app is directed to users 18 and over.

Changes to this policy
